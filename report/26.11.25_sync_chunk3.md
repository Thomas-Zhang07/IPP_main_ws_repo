### 3.3 Acoustic phase with a receiver transducer
- Receiver: a spare 40 kHz transducer on a stand at the combined-aperture centre, AC-coupled into the scope; normal mode, all-on frame.
- At trim 127: primary only 1.88 Vpp, secondary only 2.72 Vpp, both 4.40 Vpp. The in-phase sum is 4.60 Vpp, so the residual inter-board phase error is ≈34.5° — the boards add, they do not cancel.
- The 180° seen on the driver pads was a probe-polarity artifact, not a real phase error.
- Trim sweep: best trim = 16 (≈32° correction), provisional. The seam amplitude is too flat to resolve the last few degrees above the measurement noise.
- The trim register resets to 127 on power-up; re-send `--trim 16` after each power cycle.
