### 3.4 Beam steering at 1 m
- Receiver fixed on the normal at 1 m; the beam was steered in β at α = 0°.
- β = 0°: 1.65 Vpp (reference); β = 20°: 0.65 Vpp (−8.1 dB); β = 60°: 0.08 Vpp (−26.3 dB).
- Steering works; the 60° level is already ~26 dB below the main beam. At 1 m the main lobe is broad because the array is still in the near field (331 mm aperture); a true far-field cut needs a larger radius.
