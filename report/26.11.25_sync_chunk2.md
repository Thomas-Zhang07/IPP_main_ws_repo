### 3.2 Sync path
- Wired `MISC_D` (PIN_120) → `MISC_A` (PIN_65) with a common ground.
- Scope (10× probes, DC): the same ≈195 ns pulse every 25.000 µs appears on both pins. The secondary needed a 10× probe; a 1× probe rounded the edge and made the delay look larger.
- Carrier lock is confirmed: the secondary's 40 kHz follows the primary's carrier-start pulse.
