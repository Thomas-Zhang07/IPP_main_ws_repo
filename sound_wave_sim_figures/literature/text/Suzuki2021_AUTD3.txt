                                                                            the workspace is within the 30 cm cube in front of the board.
                                                                            The limitation is due to the focusability of the acoustic wave.

                                                                       Third, a square wave can drive the ultrasonic transducer;
                                                                    however, unlike pulse width modulation (PWM) motor control,
                                                                    it is necessary to control not only the duty ratio but also the sig-
                                                                    nal timing (shift duration) at a sub-microsecond resolution.
