Reference: 0 dB is the ideal coherent sum of N on-axis unit emitters in the far field. At 2 m the plotted magnitude is 2|p|/N, using individual spherical paths, spreading and local element angles. Sampling: 1.5° elevation × 3° azimuth on the surface, 0.25° in the cut, 0.2 dB display precision. Narrow off-plane peaks may be missed. The single board’s flat broadside grating orders are at ±54.75° on x and y. Results are relative simulations, not calibrated SPL.
